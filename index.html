SHARKS

My Favorite Ocean Predators

Sharks are the most majestic creature in the ocean! They are ancient fish with cartilage skeletons,over 500 species, and have existed for over 450 million years, way before dinosaurs. They have unique senses, like detecting electrical feilds. They range from tiny dwark lantern sharks to massive whale sharks. Sharks are my favourite animal on land and on water!

Some shark species (like the Greenland shark) can live over 400 years.
